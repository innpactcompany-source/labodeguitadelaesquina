Cookie policy

1. What we use

When you arrive, this website stores nothing in your browser and doesn’t connect to any third-party service. We don’t use analytics or advertising cookies, which is why we don’t ask for your consent when you arrive.

The only third-party element is the Google map, on the home page and on “Getting here”. It doesn’t load until you press “Load map” in its box, as set out in the Spanish Data Protection Agency’s (AEPD) guide on the use of cookies. Until then, you see a notice and a link to open the location in Google Maps.

2. How to give or withdraw your consent

To accept the map, press “Load map” in its box. To withdraw your consent, or to give it from any page, use “Cookie settings”: it’s in the footer of every page, in the mobile menu and here:

If you withdraw your consent, the map stops loading. Any cookies Google stored while it was active can be deleted from your browser settings.

Your choice expires after 12 months; after that, the map asks for your consent again. If your browser doesn’t allow data to be stored locally, we won’t be able to remember it and we’ll treat the map as not accepted.

3. More information

How we handle your data is set out in the privacy policy, and the owner’s details in the legal notice.
