FAQ

What people ask us most

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Thursday, 8 am to midnight. Friday and Saturday, 8 am to 2 am. Sunday, 12 noon to midnight.

What can I have for breakfast or brunch?

Toast from €1.50, sandwiches from €5.50, croissants, coffee and cake. You’ll find the full menu with prices on the website.

Do I need to book?

If you’re coming as a group or have any questions, call us or message us on WhatsApp at 682 26 25 87.

Do you have a terrace?

Yes, right on Calle Benicarló.

Can I bring my dog?

On the terrace, yes. Dogs aren’t allowed inside, except assistance dogs, which are always welcome.

Do you show football?

Yes, we show the matches on TV.

Can I pay by card?

Yes, by card or in cash. We don’t accept Bizum (a Spanish mobile payment app).

Is the venue accessible?

The entrance is step-free.

Do you have Wi-Fi?

Yes, for customers.

How do I get there by public transport?

Benimaclet station (metro lines L3 and L9, tram lines L4 and L6) is about 300 metres away, around a 4-minute walk.

I have an allergy. What can I have?

Tell a member of staff and we’ll let you know the allergens in each dish.

Any other questions?

The quickest way is to call or message us on WhatsApp.
