3. Recipients

We do not share your data with third parties unless required by law. The technology providers that host and maintain the website access data only as processors, following our instructions and under the contract required by the GDPR. Google and WhatsApp process the data they receive when you use their services, under their own policies.

4. International transfers

If you turn the map on, Google processes your IP address and there may be a transfer to the United States under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. If you don’t turn it on, there is no transfer for this reason.

5. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and portability by writing to gerencia@friser.org and proving your identity. If you believe we have not handled your data properly, you can file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es (opens another website)).

6. Automated decisions

We do not make automated decisions or create profiles with your data.

7. Security and changes

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect data. If this policy changes, we will publish the new version here with its date.
